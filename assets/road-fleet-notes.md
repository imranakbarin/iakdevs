# Painted traffic fleet

Production asset: `assets/road-fleet-painted.webp` (1086 × 1448, alpha WebP, 365 KB). Built-in ImageGen generated the original transparent PNG. The production copy preserves alpha at WebP quality 91. The atlas provides front and rear views of hatchback, sedan, SUV, pickup, van and box truck. It contains original generated vehicle illustrations, not licensed product photography.

The renderer uses measured alpha-component bounds rather than equal-sized cell crops; this preserves the pickup's wider silhouette and avoids cutting tyres or importing neighbouring vehicle fragments. A bounded 24-entry cache holds resized, lightly colour-varied sprites. The existing scene reflection and lighting finish is applied during rendering. Wheel-contact height follows the road projection; curve shear is limited and anchored at tyre level. The new projected mesh remains a loading/failure fallback. Traffic identities, speeds, model lengths and overtaking behaviour are unchanged.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent production sprite atlas for a high definition illustrated driving scene. Create exactly TWELVE beautifully rendered recognizable motor vehicles, arranged in a precisely aligned grid of THREE columns and FOUR rows with generous fully transparent gutters. Each cell contains one entire isolated vehicle, centered, NO labels, NO background, NO road, NO scenery, NO shadow outside tyres, NO borders. Vehicles are sophisticated hand-painted automotive illustrations with accurate sculpted bodywork, real curved fenders, realistic tyres and metal wheels, detailed windscreens, side mirrors, lights, plates without letters, bumpers, believable reflective glossy paint. The camera is slightly elevated above the road, mostly straight toward the front or rear with only a very subtle glimpse of the vehicle's LEFT side. Every vehicle faces the same slight three-quarter direction in its row. All wheels and bumpers fully included with consistent wheel-contact baseline at 85% of each cell height. Row 1 FRONT views, left to right: a compact modern cream hatchback with rounded bonnet; a blue elegant modern sedan with longer bonnet and low roof; a green SUV with shaped bonnet and taller cabin. Row 2 FRONT views, left to right: a rust-red pickup truck with clearly separate cabin and cargo bed; an ivory delivery van with short bonnet and curved cab; a medium blue cab-and-white-box delivery truck with separate truck cab and cargo body. Row 3 matching REAR views of the same hatchback, sedan, SUV, showing believable tail lights, rear window, hatch/boot and bumper. Row 4 matching REAR views of pickup with open cargo bed, delivery van with rear doors, box truck with tall cargo doors. Make each type distinct and convincingly a real road vehicle, NOT bus-shaped boxes, NOT toy cubes, NOT logos, NOT photographic realism. Match rich natural painterly detail of an illustrated countryside rather than plastic cartoon shading. Each cell has no vehicle overlap into neighbouring cells. Vehicle graphics should be large enough for crisp small-screen rendering. 3:4 atlas aspect ratio, high resolution, true alpha transparent background.
